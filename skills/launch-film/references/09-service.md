# 09 · Running this as a service (custom launch films)

Use this to run custom launch films for clients with the same pipeline.

## Client intake form (send before quoting)
1. Product name, URL, one-sentence description, launch date.
2. Audience (users, investors, both) and where the film will play (landing hero, social, pitch, event).
3. The one thing you want viewers to feel or believe.
4. Top 5–8 features, ranked, with a real example of each.
5. Screens: 1920×1080 dark-mode captures of each feature with realistic data, plus logo, wordmark, mascot (3D model or PNG), brand colours and fonts.
6. Proof you may show: metrics, customers, press, market numbers with sources.
7. Competitors and how you're different, in one sentence.
8. Voice: an ElevenLabs voice ID, or pick from 3 we send; gender, age, warmth.
9. Length: 30, 60 or 90 s; vertical cut-downs needed?
10. Must-say and must-not-say lines; legal or claims constraints.

## Deliverables (standard)
- A concept page with 2–3 ideas, then the full script and beat sheet for approval.
- A 16:9 4K60 master and a 1080p copy, with three score variants.
- One revision round on picture and two on audio (audio rounds are cheap: re-mux, no re-render).
- Optional add-ons:
  - a 9:16 cut-down (re-compose; don't crop);
  - captions file;
  - stems (voice / music / sfx);
  - an extra language voice.

## Cost and time to budget per film
- **API credits (ElevenLabs):** voice takes, 6–12 music takes, and about 14 sound effects (reusable). Check the account tier first: the starter tier blocks 192 kbps and concurrency.
- **Render:** about 7 min per 4K pass for a 2-minute film. Your time is the real cost: a first cut takes a long day, and shipping took about 15 rounds on the first film. Later films reuse the kit and should take 2–4 rounds.
- **Pricing:** this is a production (a concept, a script, an original or edited score, a 4K master, variants, revision rounds), not a template. Single-digit or low-double-digit prices undervalue it.
